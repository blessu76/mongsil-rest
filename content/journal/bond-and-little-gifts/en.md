desc: "The four bond stages, and the gifts and special days that come once you are friends."
---
Your bond shows how close you and Mongsil have become. There are four stages: shy, friend, best friend and family. In Mongsil's notebook you can see the current stage and how far it is to the next one.

Petting, brushing and bedtime stories bring you closer. If you are away for a few days, your bond does not go down.

Once you are friends, Mongsil starts giving you gifts. You get one when the stage goes up, and after that a new one comes three days after you open the last. There are four gifts: a crayon drawing, a little bouquet, a round pebble and a seashell. Gifts you receive can be placed in the room from the decorate screen. Once you have all four, Mongsil gives you a heart instead.

When you become best friends, the fourth bedtime story, "Starlight Train", opens. If you send a Mongsil on a trip after it has become family, your collection shows "Was family".

The 7th, 30th and 100th days after hatching are special days. On those days Mongsil wears a party hat, and a keepsake postcard is kept in its notebook.

We are still building this, so the stages and gifts may change.
