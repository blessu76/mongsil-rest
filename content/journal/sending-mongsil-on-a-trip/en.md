Mongsil grows from an egg to a baby, then a child, then a grown-up. Depending on the care it got the most, it grows into one of twelve looks.

Thirty minutes after Mongsil becomes a grown-up, you can "Send on a trip". You do not have to. Choose "Stay a while" and you can keep living together.

When you send Mongsil on a trip, it is recorded in your collection and a new egg arrives. The collection has twelve spaces. For each look you have met, it keeps the day you first met, how many times you raised it, and the name of your last friend. Spaces you have not met yet show a small hint about which care leads there.

Fill all twelve spaces and you get a Rainbow frame that you can place in the room from the decorate screen.

We are still building this, so the collection and its records may change.
