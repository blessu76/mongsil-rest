---
title: "Mongsil stays even when you are away"
desc: "How Mongsil grows while the app is closed for days."
---
You can close the app and leave it for days, and Mongsil will still be there. Mongsil does not die.

While the app is closed, time keeps passing. When you open it again, Mongsil catches up on the growth it made in that time. Catch-up counts at most eight hours, so being away longer does not make Mongsil grow any more.

If you were away for ten minutes or more, a short card called "While you were away" shows what happened while you were gone.
