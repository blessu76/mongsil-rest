---
title: "Sulking has no penalty"
desc: "How Mongsil looks when it sulks, and how it cheers up again."
---
When Mongsil sulks, that is a moment in the game, not a punishment. While the app is open, if hunger, cleanliness or mood drops to zero, Mongsil sulks and falls asleep.

While it sulks, its needs stop dropping. Pet it six times and it cheers up at once. Trying to feed or wash it also cheers it up. There is no other penalty, and no red warning or countdown.

We are still building this part.
