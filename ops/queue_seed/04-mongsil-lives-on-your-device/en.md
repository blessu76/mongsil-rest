---
title: "Mongsil lives on your device"
desc: "Where Mongsil keeps its record, and why there is no account."
---
Mongsil has no account and no ads. Its record is kept on your device.

If you turn on iCloud, the record is kept in step between your devices through your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to ipe.

We are still building this, so the details may change.
