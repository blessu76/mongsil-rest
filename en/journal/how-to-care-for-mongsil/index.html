How to look after Mongsil

In Mongsil, you can look after your pet in several ways.

  • Feed: When Mongsil is hungry, give it food. Mongsil has favourite foods, too.
  • Wash: When Mongsil gets dirty, scrub it clean.
  • Play: Play a star-catching game together under the night sky.
  • Put to bed: You can put Mongsil to sleep by hand.
  • Turn on the light: If Mongsil is asleep, turning on the light wakes it up.
  • Pet: You can pet Mongsil any time.

We are still building this, so the screens and the ways of caring may change. We will tell you here when they do.
