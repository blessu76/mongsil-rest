Sulking has no penalty

When Mongsil sulks, that is a moment in the game, not a punishment. While the app is open, if hunger, cleanliness or mood drops to zero, Mongsil sulks and falls asleep.

While it sulks, its needs stop dropping, but it grows more slowly, at half speed. Every sixth pat (counted over all the pats) cheers it up at once. Feeding, washing or playing with it also cheers it up. If nothing does, it cheers up by itself after two hours.

There is no other penalty. Mongsil does not die or leave, and there is no red warning or countdown.
