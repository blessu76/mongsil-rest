Just three things for today

Once Mongsil has hatched, a card called "Today" appears at the top of its room. It has three boxes.

  • Feed
  • Wash or brush
  • Pet or play

When all three are ticked, you see "All done today!" and your streak grows by one day. When the date changes, the boxes are empty again.

If you miss a day, once a week it counts as a rest day and your streak carries on. If you are away longer, the streak goes back to "Start again!". Even then, Mongsil does not get ill or leave, and nothing is taken away.

This time, two new kinds of care were added.

  • Brushing: After a wash, Mongsil asks "Brush me?". Stroke it gently and its fur starts to shine. You can brush it once every few hours.
  • Snacks: Where you choose food, there is a "Snacks" tab. Mongsil can have up to three snacks a day. If you try to give more, it just says it is full, and it does not get a tummy ache.

We are still building this, so the boxes and the numbers may change.
