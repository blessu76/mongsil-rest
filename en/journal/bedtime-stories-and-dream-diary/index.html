Bedtime stories make a dream diary

After 7 pm, when you go to put Mongsil to bed, it first asks which story you would like to read. Three stories are open from the start: "Cloud Blanket", "The Moon's Walk" and "Sleepy Forest". The fourth, "Starlight Train", opens when you and Mongsil become best friends. You can also choose "Just sleep" and skip the story.

You can read one story a night. The morning after a story, Mongsil is in a better mood and feels a little closer to you.

If Mongsil falls asleep between 8 pm and 6 am, that is its night sleep. After a night sleep, Mongsil wakes up once it is past 6 am and writes one line in its dream diary about last night's dream. The dream follows the care it had that day.

  • A night with a story: walked through a storybook land
  • A day of meals and snacks: ate lots of yummy things
  • A day of petting and play: played and ran around
  • A day with a wash or a brush: had a bubbly bubble bath
  • A day with no care recorded: fell asleep counting stars

You can read the dream diary in Mongsil's notebook. It keeps the ten most recent dreams.

We are still building this, so the stories and dreams may change.
