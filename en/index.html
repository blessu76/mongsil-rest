Take care of Mongsil, slowly

Mongsil is a pet care game where your pet never dies. Feed, wash, play and put Mongsil to bed, and it grows little by little. Leave it alone for a few days and Mongsil will still be there.

More about the game

What it is

  • Care without death

    If a need goes unmet, Mongsil just falls asleep. No punishments, no warnings, no countdowns.

  • Care becomes a record

    How you care for Mongsil builds up and shapes its personality and look.

  • Your own pace

    A few minutes a day is fine. Leave it for 8 hours and you lose nothing.
