About

Mongsil is a pet care game made by ipe(). You look after a pet like a Tamagotchi, but the pet never dies. The game does not rush you, and missing a day is not punished.

What you do

  • Feed, wash, play, put to bed, turn on the light and pet Mongsil.
  • Mongsil starts as an egg and grows into a baby, a child and an adult.
  • How you care for Mongsil shapes its personality and look.
  • A graduated Mongsil stays in the collection.

Made without ads

There are no ads in Mongsil. You download the app and start right away.

Languages

English and Korean come first. More languages will be added one by one.
